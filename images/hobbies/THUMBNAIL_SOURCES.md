# Hobbies thumbnail sources

The hobbies page uses local copies of official promotional artwork and film/game stills. These are images of the named works, not Caleb's own artwork or images extracted from the design mockups. Their artwork and characters belong to their respective creators and publishers. The links below record provenance; they are not automatic browser requests or a claim of ownership or endorsement.

The staged source candidates were visually inspected during integration on 2026-09-19. The selected raster files were copied without modification. The existing `cyberpunk-2077.jpg` is a previously prepared 640 × 360 export and was retained unchanged. Display cropping is handled by CSS; originals are not stretched or overwritten.
