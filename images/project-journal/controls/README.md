# Project Journal control artwork

These SVGs are original, hand-authored site artwork extending the stepped pixel style of the journal's homepage house. They contain no downloaded or third-party assets.

- `back.svg` (24 × 24): green raised disk and left arrow.
- `home.svg` (24 × 24): red-roofed house with a blue window and door.
- `favorites.svg` (24 × 24): gold star with a dark edge and pale highlights.
- `select-arrow.svg` (18 × 18): raised dropdown arrow for the status selector and the project list's scroll-down button.
- `scroll-up.svg` (18 × 18): vertically mirrored `select-arrow.svg` for the project list's scroll-up button.
- `close.svg` (12 × 12): stepped cross, used by the Information Bar's close button.
- `retro-screen.svg` (16 × 16): beige monitor with a scanlined blue screen, the status-bar icon for the retro screen effect.
- `infobar-info.svg` (16 × 16): blue information disc for the Information Bar.

The journal uses `select-arrow.svg`, `scroll-up.svg`, `close.svg`, `retro-screen.svg`, and `infobar-info.svg`; Firefox cannot style scrollbar buttons and keeps colour-only scrollbars. The Back, Home, and Favorites assets remain as dormant source artwork; the shared browser header uses its common artwork and styling.

Each asset is drawn on its native pixel grid using integer-coordinate rectangles and stepped paths. Keep the listed display size, or an integer multiple, to preserve the intended edges. The SVGs contain no scripts, external resources, fonts, or filters.

The site's [artwork terms](../../../ARTWORK_TERMS.md) remain unchanged. Their sharing permission applies to the specifically listed Hobbies decorations; this folder does not extend that permission or grant rights to Microsoft's separate artwork.
