Work

Production document systems

IntellPro · Production engineering

I contribute across a production document-processing pipeline that handles approximately 3,000 documents per day across more than 10 integrations. My work includes scalable text-extraction and document-transformation services, a custom financial-document chunking algorithm and supporting library, and ownership of the distributed embedding stage.

That work includes rate limiting, retries, worker orchestration, concurrency control, dead-letter queue handling, state-machine-based job coordination, and agent workflows for metadata extraction.

Research notes · September 2026

Currently investigating

How should lexical and semantic scores be combined when their score distributions differ?

I’m studying the foundations of hybrid fusion, focusing on score normalization and how different score distributions affect ranking.

Current project — Anime MCP

I’m building an MCP server for anime-character search. I’m currently developing its evaluation and lexical-retrieval foundation while studying hybrid fusion and relevance calibration before implementing embeddings.

Next to investigate

  • Relevance-probability calibration
  • Query-adaptive fusion

Papers guiding this work

  • An Analysis of Fusion Functions for Hybrid Retrieval
  • Score distribution models: assumptions, intuition, and robustness to score manipulation
  • Classifier Calibration: A survey on how to assess and improve predicted class probabilities
  • Query Performance Prediction for Neural IR: Are We There Yet?
