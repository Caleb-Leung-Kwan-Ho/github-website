About

I am a software engineer interested in AI and working across the agent and data layers of production systems. My work focuses on backend engineering, distributed processing, and reliable data pipelines.

At IntellPro, my work includes designing scalable text-extraction and document-transformation services, developing a custom financial-document chunking algorithm and supporting library, and owning the distributed embedding stage—where I built its rate limiting, retries, worker orchestration, concurrency control, dead-letter queue handling, and state-machine-based job coordination. I have also applied agent workflows specifically to metadata extraction.

These contributions are part of a broader production document-processing pipeline that I work on alongside the team. The pipeline handles approximately 3,000 documents per day across more than 10 integrations.

As a first-generation college graduate, I earned a B.S. in Mathematics with a concentration in Financial Mathematics and a minor in Economics from Baruch College. I am currently pursuing an M.S. in Computer Science from Georgia Tech.

Beyond Tech

Beyond my technical expertise, I bring valuable soft skills from more than 4 years in luxury and retail and 1 year in dining. I was a top seller in each retail position, working with Giorgio Armani, AllSaints, and The North Face UE in Hong Kong.

I also worked on crossover collections involving Mastermind Japan, Sacai, and THE NORTH FACE Japan (Goldwin). These experiences strengthened my customer service, sales, team collaboration, adaptability, problem-solving, and communication skills.
