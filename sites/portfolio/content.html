Welcome to my portfolio

Caleb (Kwan Ho) Leung

Software engineer specializing in AI document processing and retrieval systems.

Building reliable systems to process, organize, and retrieve information for AI applications.

I am a software engineer working across the agent and data layers of production systems. My work focuses on backend engineering, distributed processing, and reliable data pipelines.

Core ownership at IntellPro

  • Distributed embedding stage: I own the stage and built its rate limiting, retries, worker orchestration, concurrency control, dead-letter queue handling, and state-machine-based job coordination.
  • Text-extraction service: I built the service and designed scalable document-transformation services.
  • Document chunking: I designed and developed a custom financial-document chunking algorithm and supporting library that replaced the prior LangChain-based chunking implementation.

These owned components are part of a broader, team-built production document-processing pipeline that I contribute to. The pipeline handles approximately 3,000 documents per day across more than 10 integrations.

As a first-generation college graduate, I earned a B.S. in Mathematics with a concentration in Financial Mathematics and a minor in Economics from Baruch College. I am currently pursuing an M.S. in Computer Science from Georgia Tech.

I’m building a retrieval system for anime-character information, exposed through an MCP server. My current focus is indexing and retrieval, with ranking, hybrid fusion, and context expansion as areas of development and evaluation.
